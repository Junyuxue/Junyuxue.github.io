About me

Junyu is currently working toward a doctoral degree at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), under the supervision of Prof. Yi Wang. Prior to this, he obtained his B.E. from the Department of Computer Science and Technology at China University of Petroleum (East China), where he was advised by Prof. Tao Song. He was born and raised in Linyi, Shandong Province, China.

Research Interests

Keywords: Computer Networks (esp., Data Center Networks, RDMA), Machine Learning Systems, Applied AI (esp., AI for Networking/Sustainability)

He has recently focused on applying AI to real-world systems (data centers and large office buildings), leveraging deep learning, reinforcement learning and LLMs for energy efficiency. He is also working on networked systems for LLMs, with an emphasis on LLM inference serving optimization. His mission is trying to do something that is practical and applicable to existing network systems.
