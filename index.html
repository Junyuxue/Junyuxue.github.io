Research Interests

Keywords: Computer Networks (TSN, DCN, CPN), Applied AI (AI for networking/sustainability)

My EngD topic mainly focuses on the computing power network (especially the determinism, i.e. applying time-sensitive network/deterministic network). Otherwise, I am interested in the application of AI in different areas, e.g. reducing energy consumption in smart home/data center by AI technologies. I am also aware of the development of LLMs and AGI and looking for possible research junctions.

In addition to academic issues, I also pay attention to the practical application of various technologies in the real world. I am actively exploring the combination of academic and engineering (they should not be confused).
